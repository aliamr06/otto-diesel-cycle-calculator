Known parameters

Blank = unknown. Typical minimum: P₁, T₁, r (plus r꜀ for Diesel) and T₃ or qᵢₙ gives the full cycle; add bore and stroke (or V₂, Vd or mass) to get actual volumes. V₂ is the clearance volume, V₁ − V₂ the swept volume. Efficiency, net work or q values can replace r, T₃ or r꜀.

Cp = 1.005, Cv = 0.718, R = 0.287 kJ/(kg·K), constant. Cycle: 1→2 isentropic compression, 2→3 heat addition (Otto: constant volume, Diesel: constant pressure), 3→4 isentropic expansion, 4→1 heat rejection at constant volume.
