Cycle & known parameters

Use 0 for unknowns. Pressures are absolute (kPa), temperatures are K, ratios are dimensionless, and heat values are kJ/kg. Air properties are treated as constant.

Model: ideal air-standard cycle; Cp = 1.005 and Cv = 0.718 kJ/(kg·K). The solver reports variables that cannot be uniquely determined.

How to read the result

The four states are: 1→2 isentropic compression, 2→3 heat addition, 3→4 isentropic expansion, and 4→1 heat rejection. Otto adds heat at constant volume; Diesel adds heat at constant pressure.

If the inputs do not uniquely define the cycle, the calculator lists the missing properties. Use additional independent values to determine them. Values must describe a physically consistent engine cycle.

Educational calculator only. Real engines differ from ideal air-standard cycles. Chart.js is loaded from a CDN, so an internet connection is required to render the charts.
